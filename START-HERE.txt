   and PDF looks "missing" and "broken".

2. Open the extracted folder. It must look like this (all side by side):

     certificates/   (5 original certificates + thumbs/ folder)
     projects/       (calculator-app, student-management-system, smart-ai-study-assistant)
     resume/         (Anurag-Kumar-Resume-ATS.pdf, Anurag-Kumar-Resume-Premium.pdf)

3. Double-click index.html (it opens in your browser).

4. Never move index.html out of this folder. Keep the folders next to it.

   (certificates, projects, resume) to the root of the repository.
